    Spare disk

    Keep data, get paid

    This device can provide storage: it takes open storage deals whose data it can fetch, keeps a copy, and proves every round that it still holds it. Each proven round pays. Its collateral is at stake: a missed round costs part of it, an abandoned deal all of it. Best on a computer that stays on.
