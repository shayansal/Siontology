# Code of Conduct

Siontology adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is at that address; it applies to
every project space (the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and any chat or event run for the project) and to anyone
representing the project elsewhere.

In short:

- **Be respectful.** Welcome people of every background and level of
  experience. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- **Be constructive.** Give and accept feedback gracefully, take
  responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the project.
- **Do not harass.** No sexualized language or imagery, no trolling,
  insults or personal attacks, no public or private harassment, and no
  publishing of others' private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@siontology.org**. (This address
is a placeholder until the project announces its conduct contact; until
then, contact a maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS` privately.)

Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers, who will review
them promptly and fairly. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban,
depending on the impact of the behaviour.
