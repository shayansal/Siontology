Privacy

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking

    The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking code. There are no forms, accounts or newsletters.

  • No third-party requests

    Every font, script, stylesheet and image is served from this site. Fonts are not loaded from a font service, and pages embed nothing from other sites.

  • One setting, stored only on your device

    If you switch between light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under the key siontology-theme, so the next page opens the same way. It never leaves your device, and nothing is stored unless you use the switch. Clearing this site’s data removes it.
